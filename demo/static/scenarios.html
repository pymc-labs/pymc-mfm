Which channels drive sales?

Both models receive the same 166 weeks of sales, spend and promotion. They estimate each channel's average contribution to sales in that observed period. River also receives the next week's spend and promotion, but not its sales, as required by its API.

Each point is the posterior mean of a channel's average weekly contribution in the observed period; whiskers show its central 80% interval. MAE compares means with simulated direct contributions, averaged over the six channels. Fair CRPS scores the whole posterior distribution of each channel's 166-week average, then averages the six scores. Lower is better. Fair CRPS reduces finite-ensemble bias for the different draw counts (River 128; tyMC 4,000). The tyMC fit uses four chains, 1,000 warmup and 1,000 draws; check diagnostics before interpreting results.

Hand-built synthetic worlds, not a representative benchmark or real-world causal validation. The highlighted examples were selected using forecast errors on a later 42-week period, before this contribution comparison. In interaction scenarios the additional interaction term is not assigned to either channel in the simulated direct-contribution truth; estimates may use a different allocation.
