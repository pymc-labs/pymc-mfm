RMSE compares each posterior mean with observed held-out sales. CRPS scores the full distribution against the simulator’s known noise-free expected sales; lower is better. Shaded bands show central 80% intervals for latent expected sales. River does not model observation noise, so these are not posterior-predictive intervals for observed sales. The fair CRPS corrects for the different numbers of draws (River 128; tyMC 4,000). The browser fit uses four chains, 1,000 warmup and 1,000 draws. Check diagnostics before interpreting a result.

These are six hand-built synthetic examples, not evidence of performance on actual campaigns. The generating mechanisms and future inputs are known; future sales are withheld from both models. Forecast accuracy does not establish causal attribution. Whitepaper examples · Earlier model-check example
